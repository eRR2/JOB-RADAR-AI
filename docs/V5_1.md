## Changes

- Supported Codex web search and page-open/read are permitted for job-specific research,
  respecting active publisher restrictions, robots and independent-source boundaries.
- Targeted completion acts on retained opportunities without rerunning discovery.
  Full descriptions use actual controlled publisher responses and exact identity checks.
  Original LinkedIn ID/URL survives alternative publications. Unrelated titles are rejected.
  Original publisher pages can corroborate a display brand versus structured hiring
  organization; both names and the identity explanation are retained.
- Native CLI completed `open_page` events establish page-tool activity. They export URL,
  title and line-count metadata, not the complete source body. Model text therefore does
  not become a retrieved description; controlled text/JSON-LD supplies that evidence.
- Country is verified, inferred or unknown. Actual locality and generic user-established
  city/country pairs can support inferred views; new AI research also labels strong city
  mapping separately. HQ, domain suffix and search target are not country evidence.
  Inferred/unknown feedback remains unclassified and cannot teach a definitive country.
  Regional feedback is omitted from assessments where country is not verified.
- Promising — Needs Verification is the default view, separate from fully Recommended.
  Cards/details expose observed evidence, gaps, save/feedback and follow-up actions.
  Reports link stored promising jobs instead of requiring a search for narrative names.
- CV → career text → Search with AI replaces compulsory summary approval. Interpretation
  stays internal, retains exact provenance, uses original text without reinstating old
  preferences, and asks only essential ambiguity. No technical preference controls return.
- Availability distinguishes publisher-confirmed open/closed from
  **Güncellik doğrulanmadı**. Publication date is separate from discovery date.
  A validated closing date with a job-specific application URL supports open status;
  expired publisher validity supports closed. 404/410 alone do not prove job closure.
- Subscription usage, model access, provider rate limits/execution, publisher restrictions
  and time expiration have separate diagnostics. No provider or publisher bypass/retry.

## Limits

The CLI's native page body is not exported in its event stream. Controlled retrieval can
still fail on removed pages, restrictive publishers, unsupported HTML or ambiguous
cross-source identities; those observations remain preliminary. No live closed vacancy
was manufactured: expired-date handling is fixture-tested, while actual 404/410 examples
remain availability-unknown. Generic city inference is labeled inference, not proof.
Browser automation previously reported localhost `ERR_BLOCKED_BY_CLIENT`; actual HTTP
state and DOM/handler checks establish routing/visibility logic, not visual/accessibility QA.
Clean Windows VM and Claude page research remain unverified. V5.1 keeps generation-5
data and consent-based private runtime/CLI setup; no forced upgrade or stable release.
